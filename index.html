A panel quote is not a complete service plan until you know where the outdoor shutoff will sit.

Twin Cities homeowners get billed for a “panel upgrade” and only later learn an outdoor emergency disconnect, a meter-main, and a utility outage were always part of the job. Use this list before you book.

  • Ask if the outdoor emergency disconnect is a meter-main or a separate outdoor box.
  • Ask whether a same-amp panel swap still requires that outdoor shutoff under the code the inspector is using.
  • Ask who calls Xcel Energy or your co-op and how long the house will be dark.
  • Ask how the disconnect will be labeled so firefighters can find it without entering the house.
  • Ask who pulls the Minnesota electrical permit and which city or state inspector sees the work.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website and run through this list.
